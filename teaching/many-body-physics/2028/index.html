Many-Body Physics, Spring 2028

Course description

This course introduces the foundational methods of many-body theory for the study of interacting quantum systems, with a particular focus on applications in condensed matter physics. Starting from the second quantization framework, the course develops Green’s function techniques as the primary engine for describing excitations, interactions, and observable properties which can go beyond the mean-field level. With a strong methodological focus, the course equips students with the modern tools necessary to tackle contemporary challenges in condensed matter and related disciplines.
